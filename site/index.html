An independent project

Behind
OverTone.

OverTone is created and directed by Cesare Pillai. The aim is to make working with language models feel natural, with local use and a carefully considered experience at its core.

This is an early development preview. Feedback on the experience, clear bug reports, and thoughtful contributions are welcome.

Community and contributions

A few things to know.

Do I need to know which model to choose?

No. OverTone gives you a starting selection of models suited to your Mac, then suggests models based on how you use OverTone.

Does everything run locally?

When you use a local model, prompts and replies are processed on your Mac. Internet access is off by default. You decide whether to allow catalogue access, downloads, or a connection to a remote server. A remote server follows the policies of its provider.

Is it free?

OverTone is free and open source under Apache 2.0, with no subscription or message quota. Model and engine licenses are separate. The models you can run depend on your Mac’s available memory and storage.

What is available in this preview?

Local chat, prompt refinement, model discovery and downloads, model suggestions, conversation history, model parameters, and personalization. Documents, Projects, and Tools have prepared interfaces, but their workflows are not implemented yet. Windows and Linux are future targets.

How can I help?

Share how the experience feels, report a reproducible bug, or discuss an idea. Please agree substantial changes with the maintainer before implementing them. Read the community guide for the details.
