Privacy, plainly.

When you use OverTone

With local models, prompts and replies are processed on your Mac. Conversations and settings are stored locally. Model personalization uses bounded local statistics and does not send learning data.

Internet access starts off. You choose whether to allow online catalogues, model or engine downloads, and remote servers. If you connect a remote server, requests are sent to that server and its provider’s policies apply.

When you visit this website

This site does not add analytics, tracking cookies or forms. Images and walkthrough videos are served as static files.

The trailer uses YouTube’s privacy-enhanced player. It loads only after you choose to play the trailer; until then, this page does not connect to YouTube. When you play it, your browser connects to YouTube/Google, which receives connection information and may use cookies or local storage under its own policies. See Google’s privacy policy.

The public site is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub receives connection information, including IP addresses, to operate and secure the service. See the GitHub privacy statement.

Links to GitHub, YouTube, and other sites take you to those services, which have their own privacy policies.
